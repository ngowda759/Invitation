# Product Constitution

## Product
Rayara Anubhava — Digital Temple Invitation

## Vision
Create a premium digital invitation that feels like entering a sacred temple experience rather than opening a conventional website.

The emotional sequence is:

**Receive invitation → enter → experience → discover → participate → share**

## Primary audience
Devotees and families receiving the invitation through WhatsApp or a direct link, with mobile as the primary device.

## Primary goals
1. Create an emotionally resonant devotional first impression.
2. Present event/programme information immediately and clearly.
3. Make temple, Guru Rayaru, seva, gallery, and location information discoverable.
4. Make sharing effortless.
5. Preserve excellent performance and accessibility.

## Non-goals for V1
- No database.
- No authentication.
- No complex admin portal.
- No dependency on Rayaramathaynk runtime APIs.
- No payment processing.
- No real-time backend.
- No uncontrolled third-party widgets.

## Core experience
1. Opening / invocation
2. Temple hero
3. Event/festival identity
4. Darshan / timings
5. Programme timeline
6. Guru Rayaru
7. Temple experience / map
8. Sevas
9. Gallery
10. Location
11. Share

## Product quality bar
The result must feel intentionally designed for a temple invitation, not like generic generated React/Tailwind UI.

## Content principle
Content must be respectful, concise, accurate, and editable from typed content modules. Agents must not invent temple facts, timings, historical claims, dates, or religious assertions.
